# Code of Conduct

Repo Repro welcomes contributors of different backgrounds, identities, and experience levels. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces maintained by the project.

Be respectful and specific. Critique the work, explain disagreements, give others room to ask questions, and accept correction. Respect other people's privacy and ownership of their work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate humiliation, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Repeated disruption and retaliation against someone who reports a concern are also not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to **[xiaozhanyi36@gmail.com](mailto:xiaozhanyi36@gmail.com)**. Include links and relevant context, and avoid sharing more personal information than necessary. Reports will be handled as privately as practical; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where action or legal obligations require disclosure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, ask someone to change their behavior, restrict participation, or apply a temporary or permanent ban according to the severity and pattern of conduct. They should explain the reason and allow relevant context to be heard when practical. Anyone directly involved in a report should step back from deciding its outcome.

Appeals may be sent to the same address with additional context. Project maintainers are accountable to this policy as participants and moderators.
